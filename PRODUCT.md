# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective students and families in Kabul who want a clear, trustworthy overview of Zahoor Afkaar Academic Center before visiting or contacting the school. They are looking for what classes are offered, where the center is located, and whether the school feels serious and supportive.

## Product Purpose

ZAAC is a static school website for Zahoor Afkaar Academic Center in Kabul, Afghanistan. It introduces the center, presents English, mathematics, science, and computer classes, and helps visitors decide whether to contact or visit the school.

## Brand Personality

Simple, respectful, focused. The site should feel calm and academic without becoming cold or corporate.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like a SaaS product, startup tool, or flashy education platform. Avoid hype, heavy gradients, generic AI-looking sections, and busy marketing copy.

## Design Principles

- Keep the page minimal enough that the school information is easy to scan.
- Use plain language that students and families can understand quickly.
- Make education feel practical: classes, teachers, location, and student outcomes.
- Preserve a quiet academic tone, not a sales-heavy tone.
- Prefer local trust and clarity over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize readable contrast, simple navigation, keyboard-friendly links, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts for mobile visitors.
