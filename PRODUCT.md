# Product Notes

## Users

Prospective students and families in Kabul who want to understand ZAAC before visiting or contacting the center.

## Purpose

Present ZAAC's in-person and online classes, show the learning environment, and make contact/location details easy to find.

## Tone

Simple, respectful, focused, and academic. Avoid hype, SaaS/startup patterns, heavy gradients, and busy marketing copy.

## Design Principles

- Keep information easy to scan.
- Use plain language.
- Prioritize classes, teachers, location, online sign-up, and student outcomes.
- Prefer local trust and clarity over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility

Use readable contrast, simple navigation, keyboard-friendly links, mobile-friendly layouts, and user-zoom-friendly pages.
